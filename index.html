Lost & found management system

Every misrouted bag, tracked from carousel to claim.

SkyTrace lets baggage services log a found bag in seconds and lets passengers search for their own - matched automatically against flight number, tag and description, so nobody has to dig through a spreadsheet.

01

Staff log the bag

Tag number, flight, colour, brand and a short description are entered at the baggage service desk the moment a bag is found.

02

The algorithm matches it

When a passenger searches, a weighted scoring model compares their details against every unclaimed bag and ranks the closest matches.

03

Staff verify & release

A claim request lands on the staff dashboard for approval before the bag is marked returned. A human always confirms the match.
